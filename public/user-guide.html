User guide

Sprouted Accounting, step by step

How to use every part of the group's accounting app, for Sprouted Roots, Sprouted Crafts and Oikazi. Each chapter covers one screen: what it is for, who can use it, and the steps for each task.

Covers the app as of November 2026Amounts in Ghana cedis (GH₵)

01

How the app is organised

One app holds the books of all three companies. Each company keeps its own books. You choose which company you are working in, and everything you see and enter belongs to that company.

Sprouted Roots

The charity arm: farmer programmes and raw material aggregation. It sees Grants, and its reports split money into restricted and unrestricted funds and track spending per project.

Sprouted Crafts and Oikazi

Cashew and cocoa processing. They hold stock, so they see Inventory, Buying, Contracts and Pre-finance.

Words the app uses

  • Money in and money out: the two sides of every entry. You will not see "debit" or "credit".
  • Draft: saved but not yet in the books. You can change it freely.
  • Post: put it in the books. A posted entry cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Void or reverse: cancel a posted entry by adding an equal and opposite one. The original stays on the record. Nothing is ever deleted.
  • Journal: the money in and money out lines an entry puts in the books. Most screens show a preview before you post.
Good to know

VAT is worked out for you at the Ghana rates: 15% VAT, 2.5% NHIL and 2.5% GETFund, all on the same amount, 20% in total. It only applies to a company registered for VAT.

03

Signing in and two-factor

Accounts are created by invitation only. There is no sign-up page.

Accept your invitation

  1. Open the email from Sprouted Accounting and click the link. It works once and expires after 48 hours.
  2. Choose a password of at least 12 characters. A sentence you will remember works well. You don't need symbols or numbers, but the app refuses passwords known to have leaked online.
  3. Type it again in Repeat it and press Set password.
  4. Press Go to sign in and sign in with your email and new password.

If the link says This link is no longer valid, ask an Owner to send a new invitation, or use Request a reset link.

Set up two-factor sign-in

Owners, Accountants and Bookkeepers must use an authenticator app, because they can post to the books. The app asks you to set it up the first time you sign in.

  1. Install an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or similar.
  2. Confirm your password in the app when asked.
  3. Scan the code on screen with the authenticator app.
  4. Type the 6-digit code it shows into Code from the app and press Verify and enable.
  5. Save your backup codes somewhere safe, away from your phone. Each one signs you in once if you lose the phone. They are shown only once.
  6. Tick I have saved these codes somewhere safe, then Go to the app.

Sign in each day

  1. Enter your email and password and press Sign in.
  2. If you use two-factor, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and press Continue. The button says Opening the app… while the app loads.
  3. Lost your phone? Press Lost your device? Use a backup code and type one of your backup codes.
Sessions

You are signed out automatically after 12 hours, so each working day starts with a fresh sign-in.

Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page press Forgotten your password?
  2. Enter your email and press Send reset link.
  3. Open the email and choose a new password. Signing in anywhere else with the old one stops working.
Locked out?

Ten wrong passwords lock the account until an Owner unlocks it. Five wrong attempts in a minute make you wait a minute. Ten wrong two-factor codes pause code entry for 15 minutes.

04

Finding your way around

Choose the company

  1. At the top of the left-hand menu, click the box showing the Current entity.
  2. Pick Sprouted Roots, Sprouted Crafts or Oikazi. Only companies you have access to are listed.
  3. Everything on screen now belongs to that company. The coloured dot shows which one you are in.

The menu

The left-hand menu lists the screens. Some appear only where they apply:

  • Tax appears only for a company registered for VAT.
  • Inventory, Buying, Contracts and Pre-finance appear only for Sprouted Crafts and Oikazi.
  • Grants appears only for Sprouted Roots.
  • Owners also see Users and Sessions under an Owner heading.

When you click a screen, its name lights up at once. A thin green bar runs along the top and the old screen fades while the new one loads. The title then reads Opening … until the screen is ready.

Getting help

  1. Click the ? at the top right of any screen.
  2. Choose User guide to open this guide at your role. It is also the last item in the left-hand menu.
  3. Choose Contact support, pick what it is about, describe what you were doing and what happened, and press Send. Your name, role, company and screen go with it, and the reply comes to your email.

On the phone page, the same two links are at the bottom: How to use this and Contact support.

Saving

Drafts of invoices and bills save themselves as you type. Everything else has its own save or post button, and a green message confirms it worked. A red message says what went wrong and how to fix it.

05

Dashboard

The first screen after signing in: a summary of the selected company and anything needing attention.

  • Headline figures for the company, and the Entity balance.
  • What payroll owes: PAYE, SSNIT and net pay still to pay, in red when past the due date.
  • Provisional tax: which quarterly instalment is next and when it falls due.
  • VAT registration threshold, for a company not yet registered: taxable sales in the last twelve months against the GH₵750,000 limit. A warning appears at 75% and again at 90%.
  • Buying agents: float still out with each agent, red when older than the age limit. Open buying goes to the Buying screen.
  • Shared contacts, with Add contact.
  • Recent activity: the latest invoices and bills. View all opens the full list.
  • Intercompany: what each group company owes the others, and the net group position.

Sales: customer invoices

Draft→Awaiting payment→PaidorVoided

Create an invoice

  1. Open Sales and press New.
  2. Choose the Contact, the customer. If they are not listed, press ＋ New contact, fill it in and they are selected for you.
  3. Set the Date and Due date. The Document no. is allocated when you post.
  4. Pick the Currency. For a currency other than cedis, the app uses the rate on file for that date. If none exists, type the rate.
  5. For a VAT-registered company, choose Domestic (VAT charged per line) or Export (zero-rated).
  6. For each line, enter the Description, Qty, Unit price, the Account it belongs to and the VAT treatment: Standard 20%, Zero-rated or Exempt. Press Add line for more.
  7. Check the totals and the Journal preview beside the form. It shows the money in and money out the invoice will post.
  8. Attach supporting documents if you have them (see Inbox and attachments).
  9. Press Post. The invoice moves to Awaiting payment.

Until you post, the invoice stays a Draft and saves itself as you type. You can leave and come back.

Record the customer's payment

  1. Open the posted invoice and find Record payment. It shows what is Outstanding.
  2. Choose the Bank account the money arrived in, the date and the Amount. Part-payments are fine.
  3. For a foreign-currency invoice, type the rate on the bank's advice into Bank rate. The app shows any exchange gain or loss under The calculation.
  4. Press Post payment. When nothing is outstanding, the invoice shows Paid.

Cancel a posted invoice

  1. Open the invoice and press Void. If a payment was recorded, press Cancel payment first.
  2. The app posts a reversing entry and marks the invoice Voided. The original stays visible.
Filed periods are locked

Once a VAT period has been filed, documents dated in it cannot be posted, changed or voided. Correct the error in an open period instead.

The GRA E-VAT box on the invoice is reserved for the future link to GRA. Nothing is sent to GRA yet.

07

Purchases: supplier bills

Same roles as Sales.

Bills work like invoices, with a few extra choices on each line for stock, grants and contracts.

Enter a bill

  1. Open Purchases, press New and choose the supplier.
  2. Enter the date, due date, currency and lines as for an invoice. The bill shows the supplier's WHT status. Withholding tax is calculated from it and taken off what you pay.
  3. For imported goods, enter the Import VAT paid at entry. A VAT-registered company recovers it; an unregistered one adds it to the cost of the goods.
  4. On each line, set the Stock choice when it applies:
    • Not stock — expense only for ordinary costs.
    • Receive a grade (creates a lot) for produce bought in. Choose the grade, where it was Received at, and optionally a lot reference, community and district.
    • Capitalise into stock (landed cost) for costs like haulage, spread per kilo over the lots you tick.
    • Selling cost of a sales contract for costs that belong to a sale.
  5. For Sprouted Roots, choose the Grant and Budget line the cost counts against. Money charged to a grant must name a budget line, or the bill will not post.
  6. Check Net payable, attach the supplier's bill, and press Post.
  7. When you pay, use Record payment exactly as for an invoice.
Tip

A bill photographed on a phone can be sent to the Inbox (chapter 12) and matched to the bill once it is entered.

08

Contacts

Customers, suppliers, farmers and donors are shared by all three companies, so a correction made once applies everywhere. Telephone numbers, mobile money numbers and addresses are encrypted and hidden until someone with permission asks to see them.

Add a contact

  1. Press Add contact on the Dashboard, or ＋ New contact inside an invoice or bill.
  2. Enter the Name and choose Customer, Supplier or Both, and a category (Farmer, Group entity or Other).
  3. Add the TIN, phone, email and address, and the withholding tax status if they are a supplier.
  4. Press Create contact.

To edit, find or switch off contacts, go to Settings → Contacts (chapter 26).

Bank

Reconcile:OwnerAccountantBookkeeper

Bring in your bank statement and agree each line to the books.

Import a statement

  1. Download the statement from your bank as a CSV file.
  2. On Bank, press Import CSV and choose the file.
  3. Tell the app which column is which using Map: date, description, amount and so on. It remembers the layout for next time.
  4. The lines appear under Imported bank lines, split into Unreconciled, Needs action and Matched.

Reconcile each line

  1. Open a line. If the app found a likely invoice or bill, it shows a Suggested match and other Candidate documents.
  2. Confirm the right one. The document is marked paid and the line moves to Matched.
  3. If nothing matches (bank charges, for example), choose an account under Code to account and press Code row.
  4. For lines that repeat every month, use Apply repeated to code them the same way again.
  5. Made a mistake? Press Undo match or Undo.
10

Payments and mobile money

Mobile money wallets, paying many farmers at once, and farmer advances. A wallet reconciles like a bank account, and its fees and levies post to charges.

Add a wallet

  1. Under Add a mobile money wallet, give it a name (e.g. "Field buying wallet") and choose the provider: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money.
  2. Press Add wallet. It becomes a bank account of the company.

Pay many farmers at once

  1. Under Pay many farmers at once you see purchases the agent left for the office to pay. Any advance has already been taken off.
  2. Tick who to pay, set Pay on and a name (e.g. "Week 12 cashew") and press Create batch.
  3. Press Export for disbursement to get the file for your provider's bulk payment service.
  4. When the provider's statement comes back, import it (below), or use Mark paid by hand with the date and fee charged.

Import a wallet statement

  1. Under Import a statement, choose the wallet, the statement format and the CSV file. The first time, an Accountant adds the provider's format with Add the provider mappings.
  2. For each line, answer What is this line? by matching it to a batch or another entry. Fees and levies post to charges automatically.

Farmers and advances

  1. Under Add a farmer, enter their name and wallet number and save.
  2. To lend before the season, use Pre-season advance: choose the farmer, amount and wallet, then Record advance.
  3. The advance is recovered automatically from what you owe the farmer for their next deliveries. Advances still to recover lists what is outstanding.
11

Inbox and attachments

A holding place for receipts, bills and other paperwork. Save the file now and match it to its transaction later. PDFs and photos are accepted, up to 10 MB each; photos are shrunk automatically.

Upload

  1. On Inbox, press Upload files to choose files, or Photograph a receipt on a phone or tablet.
  2. The files appear under Waiting to be matched. Documents sent from phones (chapter 12) arrive here too. They are labelled with their kind, amount, date and "from a phone".

Match to a transaction

  1. Press Suggest matches. The app looks for reference numbers in file names, then transactions around the same date.
  2. For each file, choose the transaction in Match to… and press Match.
  3. Matched files move to Recently attached. Click a file name to open it.

You can also attach a file directly on an invoice, bill, payment, asset or stock count. A file on a posted transaction cannot be removed: evidence stays evidence.

Require a document before posting

  1. Under When a document is required, choose a kind of transaction, such as supplier bills.
  2. Enter an amount. At or above it, that kind of transaction cannot be posted without an attachment. Leave it blank to require one on every transaction of that kind.
  3. Save the rule.
12

Sending documents from a phone

Who:StaffData entryBookkeeperAccountantOwner

Staff on the move send in supplier bills, customer invoices, expense claims and receipts from their phones. It works with no signal: items wait on the phone and go when the signal returns.

Put it on your home screen (once)

  1. Open the app's address followed by /capture in your phone's browser and sign in.
  2. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ then Add to Home screen. iPhone (Safari): tap Share then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon from now on. Staff go straight to this page when they sign in.

Send a document

  1. Choose the Company, if you work for more than one.
  2. Under What is it?, tap Supplier bill, Customer invoice, Expense claim or Receipt.
  3. Tap Take a photo, or Choose a file for a PDF. Use Take it again if it is blurred.
  4. Enter the Amount and the Date on it. An expense claim must have an amount; for the others it is optional.
  5. Add a Note if it helps finance, e.g. what an expense was for.
  6. Tap Send.

The badge at the top says Online or Offline — saving on phone. Below the form you see what is Waiting to send (with Send now), anything that Could not be sent and why, and what was Sent this week. If the session ended, a message asks you to sign in again. Your captures stay safe on the phone.

What happens next

Each document lands in that company's Inbox for finance to match or enter. Nothing reaches the books until someone in finance enters and posts it.

13

Payroll

Prepare:OwnerAccountantBookkeeper Approve and post:OwnerAccountant

Monthly pay worked out from the statutory rates: PAYE, SSNIT tier 1 and tier 2, optional tier 3, allowances, reliefs and loans. Payroll has five tabs: Pay runs, What payroll owes, People, Reports and Settings.

Draft→Approved→Posted→Payslips emailed

Set up payroll for a company (once)

  1. On the Settings tab, switch payroll on and set the Pay day.
  2. Choose how someone who joins or leaves mid-month is paid: by calendar days or working days employed.
  3. Say whether staff may pay into tier 3 through payroll, then save.
  4. Check that an Owner has verified the statutory rates (see Settings → Payroll rates). Pay can be worked out with unverified rates, but not approved.

Add an employee

  1. On People, press Add an employee.
  2. Enter the name, staff number, start date, whether they are employed as permanent staff, tax residency, SSNIT number and Email for payslips.
  3. Choose how they are Paid by (bank transfer or mobile money) and enter the account or wallet number.
  4. Enter the Basic salary a month, any tier 3 amount, and tick the reliefs they have registered with GRA (marriage, children in education, aged dependants, disability and so on).
  5. Save. Then add allowances (vehicle, accommodation and others) with Add an allowance.
  6. Optionally split their cost across projects under Cost split across projects. Shares must add up to 100%.

For a staff loan or salary advance, use Loans and advances → Lend or advance. Enter the amount, how much comes off pay each month and the first month. Repayments are deducted automatically. Casual workers cannot yet be paid through payroll; casual labour paid through a buying agent is recorded against the agent's float.

Run the month

  1. On Pay runs, choose the Pay period and press Open the pay run. Everyone employed on any day of the month is worked out.
  2. Under Who is paid what, enter any Bonus or Overtime for this month in cedis and press Save and recalculate.
  3. Click Working on a person to see their calculation step by step, or Payslip to preview it. A draft payslip is marked Provisional.
  4. Check The journal it will post. Pay is split by project, and PAYE, SSNIT, tier 2, tier 3 and net pay are each shown as money owed.
  5. An Accountant or Owner presses Approve. The figures are locked. Use Back to draft to change something.
  6. Press Post the journal. The run is now in the books, and what it owes appears under What payroll owes.
Not ready to approve?

The app refuses to approve a run while a rate it uses is unverified or missing, for example a relief someone has ticked with no rate in force. The message names it. Fix it under Settings → Payroll rates, or change the employee, then press Save and recalculate.

Send payslips

  1. On a posted run, press Email payslips at the top to email everyone. If some were sent already, the button reads "Email the N payslips not yet sent".
  2. The message lists anyone with no email address and anything that failed. Fix those and press the button again; nobody gets a duplicate.
  3. To send one person's payslip, open their Payslip and press Email it, or Download PDF to print it.

Pay what payroll owes

  1. On What payroll owes, each amount shows who it is owed to (GRA, SSNIT, the tier 2 trustee, staff) and its due date. Due dates come from the deadlines under Payroll rates, and late items turn red.
  2. Once you have paid, press Mark paid and choose the bank account. The payment posts.

Undo a posted run

Press Reverse this pay run on the run. A reversing entry dated today undoes the journal, and the month can be run again. This only works before anything the run left owing has been paid. A draft you no longer need can be removed with Discard draft.

Reports

The Reports tab shows payroll summaries, the SSNIT report, PAYE, employer cost per employee and year-to-date figures for any company you can see. Each has Export to Excel.

14

Cash flow forecast

A look ahead at cash, built from what is already in the books plus buying seasons and anything you add. Nothing here posts.

  1. The first time, press Create the baseline. The baseline is your main set of assumptions.
  2. Under Assumptions, set how many days late customers pay, the minimum cash to keep, commodity price and volume (as % of the season plan), and how many days ahead of buying floats go out.
  3. Add Buying seasons: name, dates, tonnes and price per kilo. The forecast follows the season's curve, so you see the cash needed before anything is sold.
  4. Add Payroll, rent and the rest as recurring costs, with how often and from when.
  5. Use Add a line by hand for anything the books don't know about yet, such as a vehicle purchase. Positive amounts are money in, negative amounts are money out.
  6. Read the Lowest point and whether cash falls below the minimum, per company and for the group. Each currency is also shown on its own.
  7. To test "what if", press Copy and change on a scenario and compare them under Scenarios side by side.
  8. Press Re-read the books after new entries are posted.
15

Assets and company tax

The fixed asset register, monthly depreciation, disposals, capital allowances and the company income tax computation.

Add an asset

  1. Press Add an asset. Enter what it is (e.g. "Toyota Hilux, GR-1234-26"), the cost, Bought on and In service from.
  2. Choose Straight line or Reducing balance, the useful life in months and any residual value.
  3. Record where it is, who has it, the serial or registration, and its capital allowance class.
  4. Save. Its Depreciation schedule shows the write-down month by month.

Run depreciation each month

  1. Under Run a month, choose the month and press Run and post.
  2. Every asset in service is written down in one journal. A month can only be run once.

Sell or scrap an asset

  1. Choose the asset and Dispose of it. Enter the date and what you received.
  2. The screen shows the book value today. Anything above it is a gain, anything below a loss. Press Post the disposal.

Company tax

  1. Keep Capital allowance classes up to date with the rates in the law. The app assumes none.
  2. Set the company's Tax status. An exempt entity has no computation.
  3. Under Add a tax year, enter the year, tax rate, losses brought forward and estimated liability. Provisional instalments are based on the estimate.
  4. Press Work it out. The computation adds back depreciation and disallowed expenses, takes off capital allowances and losses, and gives the chargeable income and tax.
  5. Add any adjustments, then Post the charge. The tax posts as an expense and a liability on the last day of the year.
  6. Record each provisional tax payment under Record a payment. The Dashboard shows the next one due.
16

Grants (Sprouted Roots)

Donor grants, in the donor's own currency, periods and budget lines. Tabs: Overview, Budget, Money in, Conditions and In kind & time.

Set up a grant

  1. Press New grant. Name it (e.g. "Cashew farmer livelihoods") and choose the donor and the fund it belongs to. Anything coded to the grant is coded to its fund too.
  2. Say whether it is Restricted — for this purpose only, and set the donor currency and rate.
  3. Choose how income is recognised. This is your decision, and it cannot be changed once money has come in.
  4. Set when the donor's reporting periods start, how many days after each period the report is due, and the underspend warning level.
  5. On Budget, add each budget line (e.g. "Farmer training") with its amount.

Day to day

  1. When the donor pays, go to Money in → Record money from the donor and press Record receipt.
  2. Charge costs to the grant on the bill line (chapter 7), choosing the budget line.
  3. Release deferred income with Recognise deferred income, either because eligible spending happened or because a condition was met. The amount is worked out for you.
  4. Record donor requirements under Conditions (e.g. "Baseline survey delivered") and mark them met.
  5. Record donated goods under Record an in-kind contribution, and staff hours under Allocate staff time. Choose whether to charge the time to the grant or report it only.

Report to the donor

On Overview, the Donor report shows budget against actual in the donor's periods and currency, and flags overspent or unbudgeted lines and spending well behind schedule. Press Export to Excel.

17

Intercompany

Trade between the group companies, such as Roots supplying raw cashew to Crafts. Both sides post together so the books always agree.

  1. Under New mirrored transaction, press New reference.
  2. Choose the From entity and To entity, the Amount (GHS) and a description (e.g. "Raw cashew supply").
  3. Press Create mirrored entry. An invoice posts in one company and the matching bill in the other, linked by the shared reference.

Editing one side without the other is blocked on purpose. The Group matrix shows balances between every pair of companies and flags any Mismatch. A difference caused only by exchange rates is labelled FX difference, not a mismatch. The Related-party report is ready for the accounts' disclosure note.

Tax: VAT and withholding

Shown only for a VAT-registered company.

VAT return

  1. Choose the period. The return shows VAT, NHIL and GETFund charged on sales, what is Recoverable on purchases, and the Net position payable to GRA.
  2. Click any figure to drill down to the documents behind it.

Withholding tax

19

Inventory

Counts and NRV prices:Data entryBookkeeperAccountantOwner Post movements:AccountantOwner

Stock of raw cashew and cocoa by grade and location, valued at weighted average cost per kilo. The Live stock value should always agree with the stock accounts in the books, and a warning appears if it does not.

Set up (once)

  1. Under New commodity, add e.g. "Raw cashew nuts" with its kind (which sets the quality fields), kg per bag and shrinkage tolerance %. Press Add commodity.
  2. Add its grades with Add grade.
  3. Add warehouses under New location (e.g. "Main warehouse") with Add location. A location like Goods in Transit can have an account override.

Everyday stock work

  • Receiving stock happens through a bill line (chapter 7) or a field purchase (chapter 20). Each one creates a lot recording its origin and quality.
  • Weigh-out: choose a lot, enter the weight out now and press Record weigh-out. Loss within tolerance is absorbed; loss beyond it goes to Stock Loss.
  • Move stock between locations: choose the item, from, to and quantity, then Post transfer.
  • Change a quantity, with a reason: enter the quantity (minus removes) and what happened, then Post adjustment.

Stock count

  1. Press New count, choose the location and enter what was physically counted.
  2. Press Save draft. Anyone with the role can save a draft.
  3. An Accountant reviews the differences, ticks I have reviewed the differences above and presses Confirm and post.

Period end: net realisable value

Enter the current selling price per kg for each item. Any item whose cost is above it is flagged, and Write down posts the difference.

20

Buying and field agents

Buying agents take cash floats, buy produce from farmers and record each purchase on their phone, even without signal.

Set up an agent

  1. Add the agent and the location they receive purchases at.
  2. An Owner invites them as a user with the Data entry role and access to this company. They use the field page at /field.
  3. Set the Age limit. An open float older than this turns red here and on the Dashboard.

Advance a float

Under Advance a float, choose the agent, amount and bank account. The money is owed back until produce or cash returns.

The agent's phone page (/field)

  1. The agent adds the page to their home screen like the capture page (chapter 12).
  2. For each purchase, the agent fills in the agent, float, date, farmer (name, phone, wallet, community, district), grade, bags or kg, quality, and price paid. The gazetted producer price is suggested for cocoa.
  3. The agent chooses Paid now (from the float) or Pay centrally later, takes the farmer's signature or thumbprint, and presses Save purchase.
  4. With signal, Sync sends the saved purchases. Nothing is recorded twice.

Post what the agents sent

  1. Under Synced from the field, not yet posted, check each purchase. Assign a float to any purchase that has none (Assign float…), or reject it with a reason.
  2. Tick the purchases and press Post selected. Stock is received and the float reduced.
  3. Record labour paid from the float and cash returned, then reconcile the float when it is cleared.

Pre-finance

Facilities and advances:OwnerAccountant Deliveries:BookkeeperAccountantOwner

Money borrowed from a lender and advanced to aggregators against nuts they promise to deliver. Tabs: Overview, Borrowing, Advances, Deliveries, Aggregators, Cycles and Terms.

Borrowing

  1. Under Add a facility, enter the lender, the most you may borrow, interest a year and how the year is counted.
  2. Use Draw money to record each drawdown into a bank account.
  3. Press Charge interest to bring interest up to date. It is charged as it builds up, not only when the lender collects. Record repayments as they happen.

Advance to an aggregator

  1. On Aggregators, set the most each one may owe at once with Set limit.
  2. Under Advance money to an aggregator, enter the agreed price a kilo, the date, when the nuts are promised by and the account paid from.
  3. Enter the quality the price assumes (outturn and moisture) and the price cut per point if the load is wet or the outturn differs. These terms are fixed on the advance and never change.
  4. Press Send the money. If the advance breaks a limit you are warned, and you must tick to go ahead anyway.

A load has arrived

  1. Choose which advance it is against, the date in and the waybill number.
  2. Enter the weighbridge figures, loaded and empty (or the direct weight).
  3. Enter what the sample said: moisture %, outturn and who checked it.
  4. The app works out what the load is worth, the cut for wetness or outturn, and how much of the advance it clears.
  5. Choose what happens to any balance: still owed against the next load, paid back in cash, or written off to the season. Then press Record this load.

Keep an eye on it

The Overview shows money out with aggregators, what you owe the lender, interest so far, Who is late, what quality has cost this season, and how fast money comes back. Use Set money aside against the late ones to update the provision, and Give up on it (with a reason) to write off an advance. Cycles tracks each loop of the money from drawdown to cash back. Terms holds the defaults a new advance starts with.

Settings

Topics: Company, Chart of accounts, Projects, Funds, Contacts, Asset classes, Tax rates, Payroll rates and Backups & audit. Most are for Owners; Accountants and Bookkeepers can use exchange rates, bank accounts and the chart.

Company

  • Financial year end and company details. The company type (programme or trading) is fixed.
  • Functional currency: can only be changed while nothing has been posted.
  • VAT registration: press Switch VAT on and give the Registration effective from date. VAT applies only to documents dated on or after it. Switch VAT off leaves posted documents as they were.
  • Email sender: the address invitations and payslips come from for this company. Leave it blank to use the group default.
  • Exchange rates: enter "1 unit of USD equals … GHS" with the date and source (e.g. Bank of Ghana), then press Save rate. Foreign-currency documents cannot be posted until a rate exists.
  • Bank accounts: one currency each. Name it (e.g. "Stanbic USD account") and press Add bank account.
  • Period end revaluation: choose the period and press Preview to see each foreign balance revalued at the closing rate. Then Post revaluation, and on the first day of the next period, Reverse into next period.

Chart of accounts

  1. Press Add an account. Give the number and name, the kind of account and where it Sits beneath.
  2. Use Find an account to edit one. An account with postings can be renamed but not removed. Switch it off instead, or Merge this account into another.
  3. Changes that reach history show Before you save with what will happen. Press I understand — save it to go ahead.

Projects and Funds

  • Projects: add a project with how it is paid for, the award currency and amount, and its fund. Add Budget lines. A revised budget keeps the figure first agreed, so reports show both. A closed project leaves the coding lists but stays in every report.
  • Funds: mark each as Restricted (only for what the funder agreed) or Unrestricted. Changing this moves everything that went through the fund.

Contacts, asset classes and tax rates

  • Contacts: search, edit, switch off and set withholding tax status. Changes apply to all three companies.
  • Asset classes: the depreciation rate and accounts for each kind of asset. A change applies to new assets only.
  • Tax rates: the VAT, NHIL and GETFund rates and the registration threshold. Use Put back to the Ghanaian rates to undo changes.

Payroll rates (Owner only)

PAYE bands, SSNIT, reliefs and payment deadlines. These are national law, so one set covers every company, each with the date it takes effect.

  1. Use Show the figures for the payroll of to pick a month.
  2. Each figure shows whether it is Placeholder — not verified or verified. Check it against GRA or SSNIT. If it is right, press Mark verified, say where you checked, and confirm.
  3. If it is wrong, press Correct, enter the right figure, tick I have checked these against GRA or SSNIT and save.
  4. When the law changes, press New figures from… and give the date they take effect. The old figures end the day before. Use End… only when something is abolished.
  5. Marked a figure verified by mistake? Use Unverify to make it a placeholder again. This is refused if an approved pay run used it.
  6. Use Check a calculation to work a made-up employee through the payroll step by step and compare with a GRA calculator. Nothing is saved.

Current SSNIT split: employee 5.5% (0.5% tier 1 and 5% tier 2), employer 13% tier 1. The check figures are tier 1 total 13.5% and combined 18.5%.

Backups & audit

  • Ledger exports: a nightly copy of the books. Download latest saves it.
  • Audit log: every posting, edit and void with who and when. It cannot be edited, and Chain verified confirms nothing has been tampered with.

Good habits and troubleshooting

Good habits

  • Check the company selector before entering anything.
  • Attach the paperwork to every bill and invoice. Send it from your phone the moment you get it.
  • Reconcile the bank and mobile money wallets at least weekly.
  • Enter exchange rates on the day for any foreign-currency work.
  • Mistakes are corrected by voiding or reversing and entering again. Nothing is ever deleted, and that is deliberate.
  • Never share your password or backup codes. Every action is recorded under your name.
